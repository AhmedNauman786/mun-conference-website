About MUN

What is Model United Nations?

Model United Nations is an authentic simulation of the United Nations, where students step into the shoes of diplomats and tackle the world's most pressing challenges.

Debate & Discourse

Engage in high-level debate on real-world issues. Develop critical thinking, public speaking, and negotiation skills that last a lifetime.

Diplomacy in Action

Build alliances, draft resolutions, and navigate complex international politics just like real diplomats at the UN Headquarters.

Global Network

Connect with ambitious students from around the world. Form friendships and professional relationships that span continents.

Leadership Excellence

Graduate with unmatched leadership experience. MUN alumni go on to careers in diplomacy, law, business, and public service.
